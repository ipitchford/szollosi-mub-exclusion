# Retrospective publication-intake source audit

Date: 30 September 2026. This is not a backdated discovery-stage novelty search.
The supplied review reports a broader bounded search; the publication audit
checked the decisive source relationships below. No exhaustive priority claim.

| Source | Relationship to this candidate |
|---|---|
| [Matolcsi et al., arXiv:2503.14752v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.14752v2), Conjecture 3 and Proposition 4.2; [published version](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10801-026-01506-x) | Identifies the Szöllősi exclusion target. Searches used the exact family/exclusion terminology and the named conjecture
and correction papers. No verified redundant proof of the exact target was
identified in this bounded check. This is weaker than proof of novelty. Larger
family transfer, historical program arithmetic, formal verification and external
human review are not claimed from these source checks.

The full bibliography remains in the manuscript. Bibliographic existence alone
is not contextual verification of every mathematical statement in a source.
