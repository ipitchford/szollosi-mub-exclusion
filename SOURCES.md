# Sources and antecedents

The manuscript's [bibliography](paper/sections/partS.md) contains primary
mathematical and computational sources. The [intake audit](CITATION_AUDIT.md)
records the decisive source-to-claim comparisons and their limits.

The main antecedents are Szöllősi's two-parameter family construction, Jaming
and colleagues' Fourier exclusion, and Matolcsi and colleagues' explicit
Conjecture 3 and conditional reduction. The broad discretisation strategy is
inherited; the candidate contribution is the full specified-family exclusion
with a separately implemented interval/graph checker and inspectable hulls.

The supplied review and audit are review evidence, not primary proof of the
theorem. Their reports of sampled and complete checks must retain their stated
scope. Third-party sources retain their own licences.
